Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition — Up-Down Addition

What is it?

Up-Down Addition is an interactive web app that teaches children aged 5–7 to add 9, 8, 7 and 6 to any number — quickly, confidently, and entirely in their head.

It was created by Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition using a unique teaching method that gives children a visual, memorable way to understand what's really happening when they add near-ten numbers. Once they see it, they never forget it.

The Up-Down Method

Most children are taught to add 9 by counting on. The Up-Down method teaches them something far more powerful: a pattern they can use instantly, every time.

When you add 9, the 9 needs just 1 more to become 10. So that 1 comes from the number being added to — which goes down by 1 while the 9 goes up to 10. The answer is always one ten higher and one unit lower.

9 + 5: 9 goes UP to 10, 5 goes DOWN to 4 → answer: 14
8 + 5: 8 goes UP to 10 (needs 2), 5 goes DOWN to 3 → answer: 13
7 + 5: 7 goes UP to 10 (needs 3), 5 goes DOWN to 2 → answer: 12
6 + 5: 6 goes UP to 10 (needs 4), 5 goes DOWN to 1 → answer: 11

The same rule works for any number — single digit, double digit, or larger.

What's inside the app

Stage 1 — Ball Animation (single digit) Children see two strips of billiard-style balls. When they press Show Me, the balls that need to move slide cleanly upward from the bottom strip into the top strip. The badges update to show the new numbers. Visual, physical, unforgettable.

Stage 2 — Number Animation (all levels) Two large number boxes show the question in padded format (e.g. 09 + 07). Press Show Me and the left number rolls upward to the next ten while the right number rolls downward by the same amount — making the Up-Down principle literally visible on screen.

Flash Test The question flashes on screen for 1, 2 or 3 seconds (child's choice) then disappears. The child types the answer from memory. Ten questions, scored out of 10, with a congratulations screen on completion.

Progression system Children begin with +9 and unlock +8, +7 and +6 as they complete each Flash Test. Double digit and Double + Double levels unlock after that, building confidence step by step.

Designed for ages 5–7 and children with learning difficulties Large text, big tap targets, minimal clutter, clear instructions, and a clean purple and gold colour scheme. No ads. No distractions.

How to use it

The app is a single HTML file. No installation required. Open it in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or interactive whiteboard. Works offline once downloaded.

At home: a parent and child can work through it together
In the classroom: display on a whiteboard and lead the class through each stage
Independent practice: children can work through it on their own once they know how

🎯 How Up-Down Works

When you add 9, it needs 1 more to become 10 — so 1 ball moves UP from the bottom row into the top row. The top row becomes a 10 ball!

Adding 8? 2 balls move up. Adding 7? 3 balls up. Adding 6? 4 balls up!
